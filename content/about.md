As a kid, whenever something wasn&#39;t working, I would get completely absorbed in figuring out why. I found myself spending hours troubleshooting, diving deep into solutions just for the thrill of solving the problem.

This curiosity turned into a passion for penetration testing. It&#39;s the same mindset, searching for weak spots, understanding how things work, and breaking down problems.

Now, as a Computer Science Engineering student, I specialize in cybersecurity and have also worked on several computer vision projects. Outside of tech, I pursue freelance art and serve as the captain of my university&#39;s boxing team.
